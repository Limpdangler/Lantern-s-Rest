Lantern's Rest

Choose your first hero

You start alone. Lost adventurers deeper down will join you, up to a party of four. Explore with the D-pad, or tap a spot to walk there. Press A to use whatever you're facing (doors, chests, torches, bodies), B for the party menu, and SEARCH to look for hidden traps and doors. Tap a monster that hasn't seen you to ambush it.

When a fight starts, the room becomes a battlefield. Everyone rolls initiative. On a hero's turn, tap blue squares to move and tap enemies to attack. Tap an icon on the action bar to use it (hold it to read what it does). Push enemies into fire, acid, traps or off ledges!

Settings

Change any number, then press Save. Changes start with a new run and stay saved on this phone.

Damage uses dice notation: 1d8 means roll one 8-sided die. Heroes add their ability modifier on top; monster damage includes it (1d6+2). Ability scores (STR, DEX…) set the modifiers: 10 is average, every 2 points above adds +1. AC is the number an attack must reach. RANGE is in squares (1 = must stand next to the target). SPEED is squares per turn.
